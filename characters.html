CHARACTER GUIDE

Meet the characters of GTA 6.

⭐ PROTAGONISTS

L

LUCIA

Main Protagonist

Full Name:Lucia Martin
Age:31
Origin:Cuba
Role:Leader, Planner

A Cuban-American woman with a troubled past. Lucia is the mastermind behind the duo's criminal operations. Smart, ruthless when necessary, but cares deeply about her loved ones. Inspired by Bonnie Parker.

J

JASON

Main Protagonist

Full Name:Jason Duffy
Age:33
Origin:Vice City Native
Role:Wheelman, Enforcer

A local Vice City hood who grew up in the streets. Jason is the muscle and getaway driver of the operation. Loyal to a fault, but struggles with impulse control. Inspired by Clyde Barrow.

👥 SUPPORTING CAST

V

Victor Vance

Returning Character

An older Victor Vance from GTA: Vice City. Now runs a legitimate business but remains connected to the criminal underworld. Has history with Lucia's family.

R

Ronnie

Side Character

Jason's childhood friend who now works as a mechanic. Provides vehicles, intel, and occasional backup for jobs. Comic relief character.

K

Kiki

Side Character

Former stripper turned money launderer. Helps the protagonists clean their cash through her salon business. Has a mysterious past connection to Lucia.

💀 ANTAGONISTS

M

MARCOS

Main Antagonist

A powerful drug lord who controls Vice City's eastern territories. Ruthless businessman who deals with problems permanently. Has connections to corrupt politicians and law enforcement.
