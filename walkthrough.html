COMPLETE WALKTHROUGH

Mission-by-mission guide with tips and collectible locations.

01

PROLOGUE: NEW BEGINNINGS

Introduction to Lucia and Jason

Tutorial
Duration:1-2 hours
Collectibles:0/5
Difficulty:Easy

Mission Objectives:

  • • Meet Lucia at the diner
  • • Learn basic controls (movement, driving, shooting)
  • • Escape from the police (tutorial chase)
  • • Introduction to the Wanted system
02

CHAPTER 1: FREEDOM

First major heist setup

Main Story
Duration:2-3 hours
Collectibles:3/15
Difficulty:Normal

Mission Objectives:

  • • Establish safe house in Vice Beach
  • • Meet contacts for first bigger job
  • • Optional: Find 3 hidden packages
  • • Complete the "First Score" heist
03

CHAPTER 2: RISING UP

Criminal empire building begins

Main Story
Duration:3-4 hours
Collectibles:5/20
Difficulty:Normal

Mission Objectives:

  • • Expand territory in South Beach
  • • Meet Victor Vance (legacy character)
  • • Complete vehicle theft missions
  • • Optional: Unlock 5 more safe houses
04

CHAPTER 3: THE BIG SCORE

Major heist sequence

Critical
Duration:4-5 hours
Collectibles:7/25
Difficulty:Hard

Mission Objectives:

  • • Plan the major bank heist
  • • Recruit crew members
  • • Execute the heist with multiple approaches
  • • Escape the city under heavy fire
05

FINALE: NO TURNING BACK

Final choices and consequences

Finale
Duration:2-3 hours
Collectibles:10/30
Difficulty:Very Hard

Mission Objectives:

  • • Confront the main antagonist
  • • Choose between loyalty and betrayal (multiple endings)
  • • Final confrontation with law enforcement
  • • Ending varies based on your choices throughout the game
